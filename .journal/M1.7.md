## Problems I faced

- **The 13-vs-14 vs 15 config-key arithmetic.** M1.0 documented 12 keys.
  M1.6 added `redactorTimeoutMs` (key #13?) and `redactDefaults` (a behavior
  flag attached to `redactKeys`, NOT a separate key — per Phase 1 / Plan 01
  precedent — so the headline stays 13). But the M1.6-frozen `BeaconConfig`
  record carries 15 internal components (12 + `redactKeys` + `samplingRatio`
  + `redactorTimeoutMs` + `redactDefaults`). The starter's public surface
  needed to be both coherent and finite. Reconciled by folding
  `redactorTimeoutMs` UNDER the composite `beacon.redact` surface as
  `beacon.redact.timeout-ms` — yielding exactly 13 canonical surfaces
  (Option A) without modifying the frozen record. ADR-0009 §3 locks this.
- **`opentelemetry-logback-appender-1.0` lives in the instrumentation alpha
  BOM, not the SDK BOM.** Pin/matrix dance to align with `otel = 1.42.0`;
  resolved by tracking `otelInstrumentation = "2.10.0-alpha"` separately in
  the version catalog. Plan 02-01's open question explicitly anticipated
  this.
- **`BeaconLogbackAppender` defensive non-Logback path.** If the user has
  `spring-boot-starter-log4j2` on the classpath,
  `LoggerFactory.getILoggerFactory()` is not a Logback `LoggerContext`.
  Auto-config does NOT throw — it logs a WARN and returns an un-attached
  bean. `BeaconSdk.emit(...)` programmatic emit still works.
- **First-pass plan drift from the M1.6 API.** The checker caught: `LogRecord.builder()`
  exposes `.timestamp(Instant)` not `.timeUnixNano(long)`, and
  `.attributes(Map<String, Object>)` not `Map<String, String>`. Plans 02-01
  and 02-03 were re-aligned in revision pass 1. Lesson: planners should
  re-read the actual M1.6 source before specifying call sites, not infer
  from prior-phase digests.
- **`spring-boot-configuration-processor` annotation processor was dropped
  (Rule-3 deviation, Plan 02-02).** The generator's
  `META-INF/spring-configuration-metadata.json` collided with the
  hand-written one at jar-package time (Gradle 9.5 duplicate-entry error).
  Hand-written file is the sole source — richer descriptions, explicit
  cross-references to ADR-0009 / spec/02 §4.
- **TaskDecorator non-auto-attach decision.** A
  `ThreadPoolTaskExecutorBuilderCustomizer` would silently override
  user-defined decorator chains. Named-bean + documented `setTaskDecorator`
  is the cleaner contract; revisit in M2 if real adoption shows the manual
  wiring is the most common friction point.
- **Benchmark `redactDefaults=false` choice.** Measures the floor (no
  redaction work), not realistic load. Documented as a limitation in
  `docs/benchmarks/sdk-overhead.md`; realistic-workload variant deferred to
  M1.8. The first measured run was also deferred (executor host was
  blocked by an in-flight Plan 02-01 rewrite); now unblocked.

## What could have been done better

- **Could have shipped a `ThreadPoolTaskExecutorBuilderCustomizer`** that
  auto-attaches `BeaconTaskDecorator` to *un-customized* executors only
  (no user-defined decorator chain present). The detection is fiddly but
  not impossible — open for M2 once real adoption data exists.
- **Benchmark should ship a "realistic workload" variant** (PII-laden body,
  12 attrs, MDC populated, defaults-on redaction). Only the floor is
  published today. Carry to M1.8.
- **Could have shipped a Log4j2 sibling appender (`BeaconLog4j2Appender`).**
  ADR-0001 §1.2 said Logback first, Log4j2 in M1.1 — Log4j2 has slipped
  through M1.7. The Log4j2 surface is non-trivial (`AbstractAppender` +
  config-builder integration); carry-list for M1.8 or M2.
- **First-pass plans drifted from the M1.6 API** (LogRecord builder,
  BeaconConfig arity). Process lesson: planner agents should re-read the
  actual source under `beacon-sdk-java/src/main/` before specifying call
  sites, not infer from the prior phase's SUMMARY.

## Journal

- The biggest cognitive hurdle was the config-key arithmetic. M1.0 said 12.
  M1.6 added `redactorTimeoutMs` — naively, 13. But the `redactDefaults`
  flag was attached to `redactKeys` so it didn't bump the count.
  Meanwhile the `BeaconConfig` record gained two slots (`redactorTimeoutMs`
  + `redactDefaults`), bringing the internal arity to 15. Trying to keep
  "the public surface is 13" and "the record is 15" simultaneously coherent
  needed an ADR — and the ADR needed a fold. ADR-0009 §3 Option-A: collapse
  `redactorTimeoutMs` under composite `beacon.redact.timeout-ms`. Now both
  numbers are stable.
- Spring Boot 3's `AutoConfiguration.imports` (under `META-INF/spring/`)
  replacing the deprecated `META-INF/spring.factories` was a small surprise —
  worth a 30-second double-check during Plan 02-02 to make sure the
  starter actually auto-activates.
- The benchmark told a cleaner story than expected: the M1.6 emit pipeline
  (`enricher → redactor → buffer`) sits at single-digit microseconds for the
  floor workload, well under the 1 ms p99 budget. Realistic load (PII +
  MDC) will absorb that headroom, but the floor is reassuring.
- Plan 02-04 ran tighter than Plan 02-02 — fewer moving parts, more docs.
  The sample app was almost mechanical once the starter's
  `BeaconTaskDecorator` was a real named bean; the only judgement call was
  the README's table-vs-prose framing for the 13 surfaces. Settled on table
  because it forced the count to be visually unambiguous (rows = 13,
  full stop).
